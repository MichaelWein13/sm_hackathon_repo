The shift

Localization isn't the answer. It's the raw material.

Every positioning system assumes the building is already known and answers one narrow, well-studied question: where is a person inside that known structure? FloorFlow asks what happens when the structure itself is unknown. We treat each position as one data point toward a bigger picture, and the output isn't coordinates — it's a decision-ready graph of how the building operates and where it's about to fail: the corridor that's clogging, the doorway every route funnels through, the room people can't get out of.

Where it matters most

Emergencies in unmapped space — when the floor plan is missing, outdated, or no longer true.

  • Crowd crushes, caught before they form
  • Where hospital devices really are
  • Earthquake field hospitals & pop-up shelters
  • Tracing a hazmat or biological release
